# Provenance

This is an independent experimental port of
[Hosi121/SpeakUp](https://github.com/Hosi121/SpeakUp), originally created by the
Speak Up team for HackU 2024. React views/assets and oracle snippets are adapted
from that repository; the pinned revision is in `contract/source.json`.

No license has been added to relicense the original team's work. Dependency
licenses remain those of their respective authors. TS2Mbt/Mbt2TS generated
artifacts identify their generator in the file headers.
